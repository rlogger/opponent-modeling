0s latent cloud and source means

Training episodes · PCA display of latent vectors · colors: known source objective · stars: mean z

Latent PC1 and PC2 are two display axes summarizing the 8D 0s vectors. Each dot is one training episode; each star is the projected mean for one recorded opponent type. These are not arena positions or probabilities.

How to read the latent means and predicted paths
PC1 / PC2
The two largest perpendicular directions of variation in the standardized training latents. They combine the original coordinates; they are not z[0] and z[1]. This saved projection is different from the decision-time context plot in Recorded behavior, so its numeric coordinates are not directly comparable.
Capture / Risk / Curious mean z
One complete 8D average per labeled training group, with equal weight per episode. The coordinate readout shows the original eight entries, not the two PCA scores. The model receives this full vector, held fixed throughout an imagined rollout.
Arena x / y and action arrows
Arena axes are physical coordinates in the environment's own distance units. Red is the opponent and blue is the controlled agent. Arrows show force commands, not velocity. In Equation 3, z changes the predicted red action before learned physics predicts the next state.
Recorded source / model stop / clamped actions
The recorded path is a reference for the original episode, not ground truth for another z. A model stop is the learned prediction of termination; dashed paths after it are extrapolations. Clamping supplies the same recorded red actions to all three means, removing z's route into Equation 3 physics.
Behavior labels
Colors identify the source trajectory groups, not discovered clusters. Different paths under different means show sensitivity to z, but do not establish faithful capture, risk-averse, and curious behavior.
