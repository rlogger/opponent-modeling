What do the axes, points, and colors mean?
PC1 / PC2 (principal components)
PC1 follows the largest variation in the plotted vectors; PC2 follows the next largest, perpendicular direction. Both combine many input coordinates. They are not the first two entries of z, and neither axis is inherently capture, risk, or curiosity.
Context / latent units
Context is the 8D opponent-history summary z available before an action. Its plot uses mean-centered, unscaled latent coordinates. Zero is the displayed cohort's average; a positive or negative score is not good or bad. The PCA axes were fitted after recording, even though each context uses only preceding history.
Trajectory / arena units
This is a separate projection of complete red-and-blue path shapes. Each starting position is removed and episode duration is normalized. Scores retain arena-distance scaling, but PC1 and PC2 are not physical x and y.
Nearby points and colors
Nearby points have similar projections; differences in the other dimensions may be hidden. Colors are recorded opponent-type labels, not discovered clusters. Separation does not by itself prove strategy recovery. Do not compare numeric coordinates between different PCA views.
